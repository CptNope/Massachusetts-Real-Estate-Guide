# 📊 CMA Calculator - Public Data Sources Guide

## Overview
This guide explains where to find reliable property data for Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) in Massachusetts. While professional agents use MLS systems, students and new agents can access significant public data sources for learning and practice.

---

## 🆓 FREE Public Data Sources

### 1. Local Assessor Databases ⭐ **BEST FOR PROPERTY DETAILS**

**What You Get:**
- Property characteristics (beds, baths, square footage)
- Year built and renovation history
- Assessed values (updated annually)
- Recent sale history and prices
- Tax information and parcel IDs
- Ownership history

**How to Access:**
1. Google: `[City Name] MA assessor database`
2. Examples:
   - "Boston MA assessor database"
   - "Cambridge MA property search"
   - "Worcester MA assessor online"

**Popular Massachusetts Assessor Sites:**
- Boston: https://www.cityofboston.gov/assessing/
- Cambridge: https://www.cambridgema.gov/propertydatabase
- Worcester: http://www.worcesterma.gov/assessor
- Springfield: https://www.springfield-ma.gov/cos/index.php?id=assessing
- Most municipalities have their own databases

**Tips:**
- ✅ Most accurate for property characteristics
- ✅ Official government records
- ✅ Completely free with no registration
- ❌ May be 6-12 months behind on recent sales
- ❌ Interface varies by city/town

---

### 2. Massachusetts Registry of Deeds ⭐ **BEST FOR VERIFYING SALE PRICES**

**What You Get:**
- **Official closing prices** (legal record)
- Transfer dates (exact closing dates)
- Deed information
- Mortgage amounts
- Property ownership chain

**How to Access:**
By county - each county has its own registry:

**Major County Registries:**
- **Middlesex:** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Middlesex/
- **Worcester:** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Worcester/
- **Essex:** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Essex/
- **Norfolk:** https://www.norfolkdeeds.org/
- **Suffolk (Boston):** https://www.masslandrecords.com/suffolk/
- **Plymouth:** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Plymouth/
- **Bristol:** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Bristol/
- **Hampden:** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Hampden/
- **Barnstable (Cape Cod):** https://www.masslandrecords.com/Barnstable/

**Tips:**
- ✅ Most authoritative source for sale prices
- ✅ Legal records - cannot be disputed
- ✅ Free public access
- ❌ Requires knowing exact property address or book/page number
- ❌ Interface can be technical for beginners

---

### 3. MassGIS (Massachusetts Geographic Information System)

**What You Get:**
- Property boundaries and lot sizes
- Zoning information
- Flood zones and environmental data
- Tax parcel maps
- Geographic overlays

**Access:** https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-data-layers

**Tips:**
- ✅ Excellent for understanding property boundaries
- ✅ Zoning and environmental information
- ❌ Not ideal for pricing or comparable sales
- ❌ Technical - requires GIS knowledge

## 🌐 FREE Online Real Estate Platforms (Manual Lookup)

### 4. Zillow.com ⭐ **EASIEST FOR BEGINNERS**

**What You Get:**
- Zestimate (automated valuation)
- Recent sale prices and dates
- Property details and photos
- Rental estimates (Rent Zestimate)
- Neighborhood data and trends
- **"Comparable Sales"** section

**How to Use for CMA:**
1. Search subject property address
2. Scroll to "Price & Tax History" section → see recent sales
3. Click "Comparable Sales" tab → see nearby similar properties
4. Note sale prices, dates, and property features
5. Verify data with assessor database or Registry of Deeds

**Access:** https://www.zillow.com

**Tips:**
- ✅ User-friendly interface
- ✅ Great starting point for research
- ✅ Nationwide coverage
- ⚠️ Zestimates can be inaccurate (±10-20%)
- ❌ API no longer publicly available (manual only)
- ⚠️ Verify sale prices with official sources

---

### 5. Redfin.com ⭐ **BEST FOR DAYS ON MARKET**

**What You Get:**
- Recent sales with **Days on Market (DOM)**
- Active listings
- Market statistics and trends
- Property details and history
- Walk Score and school ratings

**How to Use for CMA:**
1. Search subject property address
2. View "Sale & Tax History"
3. Check "Nearby Homes" for comparables
4. **Note DOM** - critical for CMA adjustments
5. Filter by "Sold" to find recent sales

**Access:** https://www.redfin.com

**Tips:**
- ✅ Excellent DOM data (hard to find elsewhere)
- ✅ Real-time market statistics
- ✅ Agent-friendly interface
- ❌ Coverage may vary by area

---

### 6. Realtor.com

**What You Get:**
- Recent listings and sold properties
- Property details
- Market trends
- School information

**How to Use for CMA:**
1. Search by address
2. Use "Sold" filter
3. Draw search radius around subject property
4. Filter by beds/baths/sqft to find similar properties

**Access:** https://www.realtor.com

**Tips:**
- ✅ Affiliated with NAR (National Association of Realtors)
- ✅ Good for finding comps in specific radius
- ❌ Less data than Zillow/Redfin for non-agents

## 💼 Professional/Paid Sources

### 7. MLS (Multiple Listing Service) - **REQUIRES LICENSE**

**Access Requirements:**
- Must be a licensed real estate agent
- Must join local Realtor association
- Must pay MLS membership fees

**What You Get:**
- ✅ **Complete sales data** - every MLS transaction
- ✅ Active listings with full details
- ✅ Exact Days on Market (DOM)
- ✅ Agent notes and private remarks
- ✅ Pending sales (under contract)
- ✅ Withdrawn/expired listings
- ✅ Historical data going back years

**Cost:** Typically $300-$600/year (varies by market)

**For Students:** Once you pass your licensing exam and get hired by a brokerage, you'll receive MLS access!

## 📋 Step-by-Step: Collecting Data for CMA Practice

### Workflow for Students (Using Free Sources):

**Step 1: Identify Subject Property**
- Get full address
- Record basic details if known

**Step 2: Gather Property Characteristics**
- Go to local **Assessor Database**
- Search by address
- Record:
  - Bedrooms
  - Bathrooms
  - Square footage
  - Year built
  - Garage (yes/no)
  - Lot size

**Step 3: Find Recent Sale Price (if applicable)**
- Check assessor database for last sale
- Verify with **Registry of Deeds** for exact price
- Note sale date

**Step 4: Find Comparable Sales**
- Go to **Zillow** or **Redfin**
- Search subject property address
- Click "Comparable Sales" or "Nearby Homes"
- Filter: Sold in last 3-6 months, within 1 mile
- Find 3-6 similar properties (same beds/baths roughly)

**Step 5: Collect Comp Data**
For each comparable, record:
- ✓ Address
- ✓ Sale price (verify with assessor/deeds if possible)
- ✓ Sale date
- ✓ Bedrooms
- ✓ Bathrooms
- ✓ Square footage
- ✓ Garage (yes/no)
- ✓ Condition (estimate: Poor/Fair/Good/Excellent)
- ✓ Age (years)
- ✓ Days on Market (from Redfin if available)

**Step 6: Enter into CMA Calculator**
- Input subject property details
- Adjust market values per your area
- Enter all 3+ comparables
- Review results

**Step 7: Analyze & Present**
- Review adjusted values
- Check if range makes sense
- Generate printed CMA report
- Present findings

## ⚠️ Important Data Verification Tips

### Always Cross-Reference:
1. **Sale Prices:** Verify Zillow/Redfin prices with Registry of Deeds
2. **Property Features:** Confirm assessor data matches current condition
3. **Sale Dates:** Ensure sales are recent (last 3-6 months ideal)
4. **Property Type:** Don't mix single-family with condos/multi-family

### Red Flags:
- ❌ Sales older than 6 months (market may have changed)
- ❌ Foreclosures/REO sales (typically below market)
- ❌ Family transfers ($1 sales, gifts)
- ❌ Properties with major damage
- ❌ Tear-down sales (land value only)

## 🚫 Why No Real-Time API Integration?

**Common Question:** "Why doesn't this calculator pull live data automatically?"

**Answer:**

1. **Zillow API Discontinued:** Zillow closed public API access in 2021
2. **Realtor.com Restricted:** Only available to licensed professionals
3. **MLS Data Protected:** Requires agent credentials and legal agreements
4. **Assessor Databases Not Standardized:** Each municipality uses different systems with no universal API
5. **Educational Focus:** Learning manual data gathering teaches critical due diligence skills

**Educational Benefit:** Real estate professionals must verify data from multiple sources. Manual data collection teaches you:
- How to spot inconsistencies
- Where official records exist
- How to verify accuracy
- Professional due diligence process

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Maximum Accuracy:
1. **Use 3-6 comparables** (not just 1-2)
2. **Recent sales only** (last 3-6 months)
3. **Similar properties** (same neighborhood tier)
4. **Within 1 mile** of subject property
5. **Adjust conservatively** (don't over-adjust)
6. **Verify everything** (cross-reference sources)
7. **Consider market trends** (rising vs. falling)
8. **Document your sources** (note where data came from)

### Common Beginner Mistakes:
- ❌ Using only Zillow without verification
- ❌ Including foreclosures with standard sales
- ❌ Using sales from different neighborhoods
- ❌ Ignoring condition differences
- ❌ Not adjusting for Days on Market
- ❌ Using outdated sales (1+ years old)
