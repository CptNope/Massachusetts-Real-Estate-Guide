### Beginner → Advanced Flow:
1. **Start**: What is real estate? (Contract basics)
2. **Foundation**: Who am I? (Broker relationship, licensing)
3. **Agency**: Who do I represent? (Agency law - CRITICAL EARLY)
4. **Ethics**: What are my duties? (Fiduciary duties, Fair Housing)
5. **Compliance**: What are the rules? (Digital, privacy, legal)
6. **Process**: How do transactions work? (Cycle, contracts, attorney role)
7. **Mechanics**: How do I price/finance? (Valuation, financing, closing)
8. **Advanced**: Special situations (Tax, multi-state, investors)
9. **Reference**: Quick lookup (Cheat sheet, scenarios)
